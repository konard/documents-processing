Read documents together, and fill once when the chat has been quiet.

The quiet window was opened after a message had been read, not when it
arrived. A passport takes the better part of a minute to read, and messages
are taken in turn, so the window of one message ran out while the next was
still being read: the form was filled on a half-collected set, and again for
each message after it. The window now opens the moment a message lands, and
the timer waits for everything that has arrived before filling.

Documents are also read beside each other rather than one after another. Two
passports sent together now take as long as the slower reading, not the sum of
both. What each reading writes into the session still goes in in turn, so two
finishing at once cannot write over each other.

The result is what an applicant expects: send as many messages as you like,
and twenty seconds after the last one the form is filled once, with everything
on it.
