Fill the form part by part, and show each part as it is done.

The form was filled field by field in schema order and shown once at the end,
so an applicant watched a typing indicator for a minute and then received the
whole thing at once. It is now filled the way it is read: the photographs, then
who they are, then the passport, the contacts, the occupation, the trip.

Each part is filled, settled and sent before the next is begun, so the chat
shows the form appearing in the order it is actually being filled. Every part
is shown, including one this fill had nothing to put in, since the applicant is
checking all of them and a part that skipped its turn reads as one that went
wrong. A part is captured by its own heading, so what is sent is exactly the
part just filled.

Which field belongs to which part is read from the page itself, so a form that
has been rearranged is still filled in its own order; the grouping read off the
live form is the fallback for a page that will not answer.
